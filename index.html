This is the transparent button sweets with flowers inside. Located at 101 Nishirokkakuchō, Nakagyo Ward — near Karasuma Oike, same neighbourhood as (THISIS)SHIZEN. Served with kuromitsu black honey and kinako soybean flour, with unlimited hojicha or matcha. ⚠️ Reservation strongly recommended — people turned away at the door without one. Book via TableCheck in advance. Opens 8:00–20:00 (Sat until 20:00). You could do SHIZEN and Rokujuan as a pair on Day 7 since they're in the same neighbourhood — allow 1 hour total for both.

Shinkansen
Register at smart-ex.jp before you leave Greece. Book Hikari (¥14,170/person (~€86) Tokyo→Kyoto). Seats D and E for Mt Fuji views on the right side. Departs from Tokyo Station or Shinagawa.
🚲
Kyoto e-bikes
Book through your Kyoto hotel on arrival. Distances and hills make e-bikes essential. Our friend is emphatic — it completely changes the Kyoto experience.
📖
Eki stamps
Buy a small notebook at any station shop on day 1. Stamp at every JR station you pass through — free, unique per city, a perfect trip-long souvenir for the kids.
📱
Essential apps
USJ Official app (Nintendo World entry), Tokyo Disney app, Google Maps (works perfectly in Japan), HyperDia or Navitime for train routes. Download before leaving Greece.
🧳
Luggage forwarding
Yamato Transport "Takkyubin" — send bags from Tokyo hotel to Kyoto hotel overnight (¥1,500–2,000/bag (~€9–12), book the night before). Travel completely light on the Shinkansen.
🌡️
August heat
32–36°C and humid. Small towel, hand fan, sunscreen every day. Visit temples before 10am or after 4pm. Cold drinks from convenience stores are your best friends all day.
📋
Japan entry QR
Register at Visit Japan Web before departure — saves time at the airport and is used for VAT refunds in shops. Bring passports when shopping for refunds.
🍬
Okashi no Machioka
Japan's discount snack chain — walls of unique treats at ¥50–300 (~€0.30–2). Perfect for cheap omiyage (gifts) for friends and colleagues back home. Branches everywhere in Tokyo and Osaka.
🚫
Street rules
No bins on the street — use 7-Eleven/Lawson bins. No smoking on streets. Eating while walking is frowned upon. Queue on the left even in the subway. All small gestures that locals notice.
🍣
Restaurant bookings
Book advance restaurants via Jpneazy or Tablecheck. Wagyu Yakiniku Waya (Kyoto), any Michelin spots — reserve weeks ahead. Most casual ramen and market spots are walk-in.
